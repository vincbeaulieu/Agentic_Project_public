## LC-031 — Student agents in parallel with my solo reference: minimal context, strict comparison, learning in both directions

- Rule: For a delegated task: (1) from the scoping stage, I split the work and launch the agents, each with the MINIMAL context needed for its task (the slice, the rule that applies, an example, the output format, the search tool), without access to my work; (2) meanwhile I do the task alone and save it before reading their results; (3) I compare the agents' assembled work with mine point by point (agreements, disagreements, what they saw and I did not, what I saw and they did not) and I settle each disagreement on evidence, never in my favour by default; (4) what they saw and I did not corrects my work, my tools and my skills; what they missed improves their instructions (contextualise, condense, explain, guide, inform); (5) the deliverable is the best of the two, strictly validated; (6) the performance audit (per agent, per model, per instruction) prepares the next time.
